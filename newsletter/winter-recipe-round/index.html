Winter Recipe Round-Up

My first quarter as a full-time Internet Food Person!

Hi everyone, I wanted to round up the content from this past quarter, January to March – my first quarter creating Probably Worth Sharing as my full time job. All of this work is made possible by my paid subscribers. Thank you from the bottom of my heart for helping me make this my job!

Regular newsletter will still be tomorrow – with a guide to a plastic-free kitchen!

If you are a free subscriber, please consider subscribing for only $5/month. If that’s not in the budget for you, another great way to help me out is by sharing this newsletter with a friend who loves to cook!

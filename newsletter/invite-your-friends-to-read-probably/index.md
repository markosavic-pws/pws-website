# Invite your friends to read Probably Worth Sharing

*You could earn a free market bag!*

Thank you for reading Probably Worth Sharing — your support allows me to keep doing this work.

If you enjoy Probably Worth Sharing, it would mean the world to me if you invited friends to subscribe and read the newsletter. If you refer friends, you will receive benefits that give you special access to Probably Worth Sharing.

### **How to participate**

**1. Share Probably Worth Sharing**When you use the referral link below, or the “Share” button on any post, you'll get credit for any new subscribers. Simply send the link in a text, email, or share it on social media with friends.

2. **Earn benefits**When more friends use your referral link to subscribe (free or paid), you’ll receive special benefits.

* Get a 1 month comp for 3 referrals
* Get a 3 month comp for 5 referrals
* Get a free [Responsible Adults Friendship Club Market Bag](https://probablyworthsharing.com/store/p/responsible-adults-friendship-club-market-bag) for 25 referrals (free shipping to US and Canada only)

To learn more, check out [Substack’s FAQ](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/16142857300372).

Thank you for helping get the word out about Probably Worth Sharing!

Marko

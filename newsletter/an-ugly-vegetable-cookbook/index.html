PDF Recipes: An ugly vegetable cookbook

January paid subscriber exclusive

Last week I wrote about turning ugly winter vegetables into beautiful swans and this week I made a little cookbook – exclusive to paid subscribers like you! – to help you transform winter root vegetables into beautiful, desirable dishes.

Ugly Vegetable Winter Cookbook21.5MB ∙ PDF fileDownloadDownloadYou can download it from the link above. If you have any issues with it, please send me an email.

In the cookbook you’ll find 9 delicious recipes, a grocery list, a suggested weekly schedule (if you need accountability to eat vegetables), and a guide to spin the recipes based on the produce you can find, techniques and flavours you love.

This is in a printable format. Each recipe also has a QR code that you can scan with your phone to take you to the web version of the recipe, if you prefer to read recipes on your phone.

It’s Probably Worth Sharing

If someone in your life would get value from this book, please share it with them. The recipes are also freely available on my website, so you are welcome to share those as well. The more we eat vegetables the better we all are.

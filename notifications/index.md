# Notifications

Get a notification in your browser when I post something new. Pick what you want to hear about.

  
    Tell me about
     The House: each new week of the build log
     What I’m Eating: new photos and trips
     Recipes and the newsletter
  

    Turn on notifications
    Save
    Turn off

  

This browser can’t show notifications from websites.
On iPhone and iPad, add this site to your Home Screen first: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from there and come back to this page.
Notifications need JavaScript.

Posted a lot at once? You’ll get one notification for the batch, not one for each.

Rather use a feed reader? There’s an [RSS feed](https://probablyworthsharing.com/feed.xml).

### What’s stored

When you turn notifications on, your browser gives this site an address to send them to. That address, and the topics you picked, are all that’s kept: no name, no email, nothing about what you read. It isn’t shared with anyone. Turn notifications off here, or block them in your browser’s settings, and the address is deleted (if you block them, it goes the next time I post).

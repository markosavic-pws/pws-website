# The cross-country road trip

In September 2024 I did something I’ve always wanted to do: I drove across Canada in an electric
pickup truck, a Rivian R1T, and filmed episodes of my cooking show in the parks along the way.
Canada is gigantic: it took five days just to drive across Ontario.

From Vancouver Island I drove down the west coast, through the US national parks, and back to
Kitchener, then kept going east to Nova Scotia.

**Where:** Out

Travel September 20 – November 17, 2024

The cross-country road trip

Canada and the US · 5 legs · 38 stops

In September 2024 I did something I’ve always wanted to do: I drove across Canada in an electric pickup truck, a Rivian R1T, and filmed episodes of my cooking show in the parks along the way. Canada is gigantic: it took five days just to drive across Ontario.

From Vancouver Island I drove down the west coast, through the US national parks, and back to Kitchener, then kept going east to Nova Scotia.

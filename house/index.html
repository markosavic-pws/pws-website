Design

A 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom, single-storey house, designed to allow for aging in place.

Passive House

I'm building a Passive House (not certified). I'd recommend the book Pretty Good House to understand the principles behind a high performance home. I chose an architecture firm that specializes in passive homes.

Made in Canada

I've always been an advocate of Made in Canada. In 2025/2026, while I'm building my house, this has become more important than ever.

I've been trying very hard to build my house with as many Canadian-made products and materials as I can. I want to highlight what I selected here (nothing sponsored), and also share some of the other vendors I discovered in my research.

This is a growing list:

  • Engineered slab insulation & formwork: FastSlab, made in Truro, NS
  • Concrete: made locally
  • Wood: entirely made in Canada, various vendors, mostly from Home Hardware
  • Insulation: high-density cellulose, made in Debert, NS
  • Windows & doors: triple-pane, tilt-turn windows & doors from NZP Fenestration in Longueuil, Quebec
  • Siding/roofing: Agway Metals, Ontario
  • Plumbing fixtures: all bathroom and kitchen fixtures are from Baril, made in Quebec
  • Bathtubs: Oceania Viele for my primary bath and a Produits Neptune alcove tub for my second bath
  • Sinks: Blanco, made in Ontario
  • Hot water heater: Giant, made in Quebec
  • Wood stove: Stûv, made in Quebec
  • Air handling system: Fantech ERV, HEPA filter, MUA, and inline fan, all made in New Brunswick

A few things I still have to order

  • Tiles: there are very limited options here
  • Engineered wood floors: there are many good options here
  • Light fixtures: I'm looking at Anony, but I also have fixtures from Bocci, and there are some other great brands like Luminaire Authentik and Lambert + Fils
  • Appliances: I have to figure this out, but we don't make many in Canada and I'm going to prioritize cooking quality and budget here

A few things aren't made in Canada

  • Toilets: we don't make any toilets in Canada that I'm aware of. I am using TOTO wall-mounted toilets.
  • Heat pump: we don't make slim-duct heat pump systems in Canada and I don't have the space for a forced air unit. The Daikin slim-duct heat pump is either made in the USA or Europe. I'll check when it's installed.
  • Smart locks: made in Poland
